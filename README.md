Connected Dreams A — Support



Welcome to the official support page for Connected Dreams A, a wellness and smart-connectivity app designed to enhance daily routines through intelligent alarms, sleep tools, and connectivity-enabled companion devices.

This public page provides help, contact information, and general guidance for users.

---

About the App

Connected Dreams A combines:
- creation of healthy sleep routines and gentle 'alarms',
- optional short journaling features (For frequent and longer entries, please visit Connected Dreams C. Connected Dreams C also provides children-friendly features.),
- wellness tracking,
- optional connectivity to smart devices using Matter, Bluetooth, and Http to communicate with nearby compatible devices, such as audio or light accessories, as part of user-configured routines. Users may connect other devices, if appropriate, for wellness or health-related tracking.


Initial release of Connected Dreams A combines healthy sleep routines, gentle alarms, short-form journaling, personalized wellness tracking, custom metric tiles, and a built-in in-app agent that can help users set alarms, review sleep progress, understand connectivity options, and update selected app data. 

Optional connectivity through Matter, Bluetooth, and HTTP allows users to communicate with compatible nearby devices, accessories, websites, and services as part of user-configured routines. Users may also connect appropriate compatible devices for general wellness or health-related awareness. 

The app includes accessibility features including microphone access to schedule alarms and basic functions.

FAQ

Why does the app request Bluetooth permission?
The app uses Bluetooth to communicate with nearby compatible Bluetooth-enabled devices as part of user-initiated routines or interactions. Users may also connect other Bluetooth devices for wellness or health-related tracking. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal data.

Why does the app request Camera access?
Camera access for:
- Scanning QR codes
- Optional Matter pairing (coming in future release) 
  We do not store or share images.

Does the app collect any personal information?
No. Connected Dreams A does not collect, store, or transmit user data.

Are scans or photos saved?
No. All scanning happens locally on your device.
